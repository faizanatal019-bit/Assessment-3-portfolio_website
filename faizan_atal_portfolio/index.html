Evidence and Reflection

How I built this site and what I learned.

I started from the Bootstrap 5 template and rebuilt the look as a dark, terminal-style theme for a security audience. I chose my own colours, fonts and icons, and replaced all of the sample content with my own work.

The page uses one column on phones and two columns on large screens. The menu collapses into a button on small screens, and the photo captions move below the photo.

Two people will test the navigation, readability and accessibility. I will record what they found and what I changed here.

Add screenshots to each lab card, add new labs as I finish them, and do more accessibility testing.
